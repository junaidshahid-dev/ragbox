# Acme Tools — Customer Handbook (sample document)

## Refund policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days,
refunds are issued as store credit only. Digital products are refundable within
14 days provided the license key has not been activated.

## Shipping

Orders placed before 3pm ship the same business day. Standard delivery takes
2-4 business days nationwide. Express delivery (next business day) is available
for a flat fee of $12.

## Warranty

All hardware carries a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage or liquid damage. Warranty claims
require the original order number.

## Support

The support desk is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm (PKT), via email and live
chat. Priority support with a 2-hour response SLA is included in the Business plan.
